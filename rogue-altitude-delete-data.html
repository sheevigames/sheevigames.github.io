Delete your Rogue Altitude data

Last updated 13 November 2026

Rogue Altitude by Shivi's Games · com.shivi.rogueAltitude

Rogue Altitude has no accounts. Your pilot name, career, XP, unlocks and match records are stored only on your device, and you can delete them yourself at any time. To ask us to delete anything else, email us as described below.

Request deletion from us

  1. Email sheevigames@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Rogue Altitude data.
  2. Tell us whether you play on Android or iOS, and include any support emails or details you have sent us before so we can find them.
  3. We will confirm by email and complete the request within 30 days.

Delete the data on your device yourself

  1. Android: Settings > Apps > Rogue Altitude > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  2. iOS: press and hold the Rogue Altitude icon > Remove App > Delete App. (Offload App keeps your data.)

This permanently removes your pilot name, career XP, levels, unlocked pilots and cosmetics, daily-contract progress, ad-bonus records, match statistics and settings from that device. Device backups you have made may restore them.

Stop future collection

  • Usage statistics: turn off Share anonymous usage stats on the ACCOUNT screen. Statistics not yet sent are discarded.
  • Ads: ads only play when you choose to watch one. Where the law requires it, change your choices with Ad privacy choices on the ACCOUNT screen. On Android you can also delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: support emails and any attachments or information you have sent us.
  • Deleted automatically: anonymous usage statistics held by Google Analytics for Firebase are deleted after 14 months. They are not linked to your name or an account, so we cannot single them out for an individual request.
  • Kept by Google: information Google AdMob uses to show and measure ads is kept for the periods in Google's privacy policy. You can manage it at My Ad Center.
  • Kept where required: we may keep a record of your request, and information we must keep by law or to resolve a dispute, only as long as needed.
