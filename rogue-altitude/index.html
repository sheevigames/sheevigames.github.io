Features

🚀

Jetpack combat

Boost, aim and dodge in fast arena fights with health, fuel and respawns.

🎯

Train against bots

Pick from zero to ten bots and practise before you face real players.

Local multiplayer over Bluetooth or the same Wi-Fi network, with no accounts.
