Website privacy and cookies

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This page explains how shivisgames.com ("the website", "we", "us") handles information. It covers the website only. Each of our apps has its own privacy policy: Boards, Rogue Altitude and MindSprint.

In short

You can use the website without an account. We only use analytics cookies if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner, and you can change your mind at any time.

Who is responsible

Shivi Games (Google Play developer name: Shivi Tech), contact hello@shivisgames.com.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

If you accept, we use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they view, where they came from (for example a search engine or social media) and which links they click, such as the Google Play buttons. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and pages visited. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not use it for advertising.

If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. Google's own information about this is at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your cookie choice

Your choice is saved in your browser so we don't ask again. To change it, reset your cookie choice and you will see the banner again on the home page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Sign-up for launch news

If you use the "Notify me at launch" form, your email address and the games you pick are stored in Google Forms on our Google account. We only use them to tell you when those games launch, then we delete them. To be removed sooner, email us.

Videos and other services

Gameplay videos come from YouTube and only load when you tap one, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Fonts come from Google Fonts. Links to Google Play, YouTube, Instagram and X take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

How long information is kept

Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted automatically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to its use. Email us and we will help.

Children

The website is for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 for analytics.

Changes to this page

We may update this page. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions or requests: hello@shivisgames.com.
