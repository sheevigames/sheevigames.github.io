Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Rogue Altitude · com.shivi.rogueAltitude

This Privacy Policy explains how Rogue Altitude, provided by Shivi's Games, handles information when you play the game, use local multiplayer, contact us or visit these policy pages.

Your career and settings are saved on your device. When you choose Bluetooth or LAN multiplayer, game information is shared with the players and host involved in that session. Support messages are handled by Shivi's Games.

Who is responsible

Shivi's Games is responsible for the information we handle under this policy. Contact us at sheevigames@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your chosen pilot name, training and multiplayer kills and deaths, match records, time played, team results, career XP, levels, ranks, daily-contract progress, unlocked pilots, cosmetic rewards and equipped items. Preferences such as sound settings may also be saved locally.

This local profile lets the game show your progress between sessions. The ACCOUNT screen manages the profile on that device. Keep a copy of information you want to retain before clearing game data or changing devices.

Bluetooth multiplayer

When you host or search for a nearby game, the app uses Bluetooth Low Energy to discover and connect to compatible devices. Hosting can advertise your chosen player or room name to nearby devices looking for a game.

During a session, connected devices exchange the information needed to run the lobby and match. This includes player names, session identifiers, selected pilots, weapons, teams, arena and match settings, gameplay inputs, fighter positions, health, projectiles and match scores. The host coordinates the session and other participants receive the relevant game state.

Bluetooth session information travels directly between the connected devices. Each participant's app can save its own local match statistics. Choose a nickname you are comfortable sharing with nearby players.

LAN multiplayer and game hosts

When you connect to a LAN game, the app sends lobby and match information to the WebSocket server address you choose. The host server coordinates players and distributes game state. Its operator can access the information sent to that server and connection information such as your IP address.

The supplied room server keeps active rooms in memory and removes a room when its final participant leaves. An operator's hosting environment, network equipment or modified server can have separate logs and retention practices. Ask the operator about those practices before connecting. Use a trusted host and network.

Session information is used to discover games, connect players, coordinate matches and show results. Your career profile, daily-contract history and cosmetic inventory are maintained in local storage.

Device permissions

  • Bluetooth and Nearby devices permissions enable hosting, discovery and connections.
  • On Android 11 and earlier, location permission is requested for Bluetooth discovery because of Android's permission requirements. Discovery uses Bluetooth signals. Geographic coordinates are outside the game's discovery process.
  • Network access and any platform-requested local-network permission enable communication with game hosts. Development builds can also connect to a local development server.

You can review or revoke permissions in your device settings. Revoking a permission can prevent the related multiplayer feature from working. Offline training remains available.

App update checks

Release builds check for an available update after the opening splash, while you are on the home screen. Android uses Google Play update services and its account, device and app eligibility checks. App Store builds request the public listing for the app identifier from Apple. TestFlight builds request a small release manifest from our GitHub Pages site and compare its build number locally.

Apple, Google or GitHub can receive connection information such as your IP address when these checks run. The checks use app version and build information to identify an applicable update. Choosing Later stores a reminder for that release on your device for three days. The update flow can open the relevant store or TestFlight when you choose Update.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots, logs or other information you choose to attach. We use that information to respond, investigate the reported problem and maintain a support record. Send only information relevant to your request.

Support messages may be processed by our email provider. Retention is limited to the time needed to resolve and follow up on the request, meet legal obligations and address related disputes. You can request deletion by contacting us, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Advertising, analytics and future features

This policy describes the current local-profile, multiplayer and update-check release. Advertising, third-party analytics and crash-reporting SDKs are absent from this release. If a future release adds advertising, analytics, purchase verification, cloud accounts or downloadable content services, we will update this policy to identify the providers, information handled, purposes and available choices. The app will provide any disclosures and permissions required for those features.

App stores and this website

Apple and Google process downloads, store interactions and any platform diagnostics you choose to share under their own privacy policies.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and handles website connection information under its privacy statement. Support email and website hosting providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, subject to their applicable safeguards.

Retention and deleting local progress

Your local profile remains in app storage until it is removed. On Android, use the app's system settings to clear its storage or uninstall it. On iOS, use Delete App to remove the installed app and its local data. Offload App preserves app data. Device backups, transfers and restored backups can retain or restore information, depending on platform settings.

Deleting local app data removes that device's career and unlock records. Other players' records and a host operator's logs are managed by those recipients. Contact the relevant operator about information they retain.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can edit your pilot name, choose whether to use multiplayer, leave a session, revoke device permissions and remove local data. Depending on your location, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information we hold, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to a data protection authority.

Email us to exercise rights concerning support information we hold. Requests concerning a separate game host should also be sent to that host's operator. Where applicable, handling support requests relies on our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the game, your requested assistance, and relevant legal obligations. Where consent is required, the relevant feature must obtain it.

Children and families

Parents and guardians can review the game's store age rating, supervise multiplayer, manage permissions and remove local progress through device settings. Players should use nicknames and share personal details with care.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support request without any required parental authorization, contact us so we can take appropriate steps, including deletion where required by law.

Security

Local information is protected by your device's security and app-storage controls. Multiplayer information is available to the connected host and participating devices as needed for the game. Transport protection depends on the connection and host configuration. A plain ws:// connection sends data without WebSocket transport encryption, while wss:// uses TLS. Keep your device secure and connect to trusted hosts.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when relevant features or information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will also be communicated through the app or store listing as appropriate.
