About the studio

Shivi Games is a small independent studio from India, run by solo developer Mohit Mali. We make free mobile games you can pick up in a minute, built for playing with friends in the same room over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi, with no accounts or sign-up needed. Our first game, Boards, brings nine classic board and puzzle games together in one app.

Boards

Chess, Ludo & Sudoku · Out now on Android
GET IT ONGoogle PlayGame page

Short description

Nine classic board games and word puzzles in one free app, playable offline or with friends nearby over Bluetooth.

About the game

Boards brings Chess, Checkers, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Carrom, Sling Puck, Sudoku, Nonogram and four word games together in one free app. Play the computer at your level, pass one phone around, or connect with friends in the same room over Bluetooth with no internet needed. A Daily Challenge, achievements and seven languages keep players coming back.

Key features

  • Nine games: classic boards, Sudoku, Nonogram and Word Puzzle
  • Computer opponents that get harder as you win
  • Bluetooth multiplayer with friends nearby, no internet needed
  • Pass-and-play on one phone
  • Daily Challenge with streaks and achievements
  • English, Hindi, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Arabic and Urdu

Rogue Altitude

Jetpack Arena · Coming soon to Android
Coming soon to Google Play🔔 Notify meGame page

Short description

Anime-style jetpack arena battles: train against bots, then fight friends in the same room.

About the game

Rogue Altitude takes the fight skyward. Boost, aim and dodge in fast jetpack battles across nine arenas, train against up to ten bots, then take on friends over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. Choose from twelve pilots across five classes, earn career XP, climb the ranks and finish daily contracts.

Key features

  • Fast jetpack arena combat
  • Train against up to ten bots
  • Local multiplayer over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, no accounts
  • 12 pilots in five classes, 9 arenas
  • Ranks, career XP and daily contracts

MindSprint

Focus & Memory · Coming soon to Android
Coming soon to Google Play🔔 Notify meGame page

Short description

Seven quick brain games in one free app, with hundreds of levels and a Daily Challenge.

About the game

MindSprint trains focus, logic, planning and memory a few minutes at a time. Sort colours in Water Sort, connect numbers in Pipe Path, plant the perfect Garden Grid, repeat the Memory Lanterns and more, across hundreds of levels that get harder as you go. Play offline, use hints when stuck and take on a Daily Challenge that grows with you.

Key features

  • Seven brain games: Water Sort, Pipe Path, Garden Grid, Memory Lanterns, Arrow Drift, Rider Rush, Hive Fill
  • Hundreds of levels that get harder as you go
  • Daily Challenge matched to your level
  • Hints when stuck
  • Play offline
